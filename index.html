Since 1974

Fifty-one years, same family, same area

J. R. Manners Roofing has been working on roofs across Sunderland and Houghton Le Spring since 1974. John Manners has over forty years on the tools himself, and Dean now runs the business alongside him.

In that time we've re-roofed houses we first worked on decades ago, for families we've known just as long. That is not a marketing line, it is in the reviews, some from customers who have used us for over thirty years.

FAQ

Good to know

How long have you been trading?

Since 1974, so over fifty years. Same family, same part of Tyne and Wear. Some of our customers have used us for more than thirty years.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We'll come out, look at the roof and give you a price, with no charge and no obligation to go ahead.

Do you guarantee the work?

All work is guaranteed. We're a local family firm that has been here fifty years, so we're not difficult to find if something needs looking at again.

What areas do you cover?

Sunderland, Houghton Le Spring, Fencehouses and the wider Tyne and Wear area.

Can you come out at short notice?

We're open six days a week plus Sunday mornings, and can be flexible outside those hours for genuine emergencies like a leak after a storm.
